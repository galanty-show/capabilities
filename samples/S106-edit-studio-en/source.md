# Fix a word. Play on. (Galanty intro film, English cut — creative notes, original)

Fix one word, render it all again? In Galanty, no waiting.

This 58-second beat-cut film covers three things, each landing on one musical section:

1. Live preview: in the sample library, hover a cover and it plays live; open the Live Player and nothing is rendered — every frame is drawn on your computer. Press C to swap palettes, drag the bar to any scene, press V to turn the voice off, use [ ] to change the pace — all of it applies instantly.
2. Precise edits: pause, and you see which scene you are in, which sentence is being said and which file draws it. Change just one word, save, and it redraws on your computer and plays on from the same moment. If a film was already exported, the export button reminds you: "New changes — export again".
3. Asset tracking: every imported asset keeps its source, author and license; bundled tracks that need credit get the credit written for you; bundled icons come with their license.

Watching, playing and editing are free.

There is no voice-over; all information is carried by on-screen text. The player, sample library, timeline, asset list and export panel are simplified redraws of the real interface, and "City of Light" and "Next week" are demo films. The score "Play On" is original and synthesized from code for this film.
